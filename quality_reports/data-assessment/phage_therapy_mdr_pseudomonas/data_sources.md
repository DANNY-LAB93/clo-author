### Detailed Note: Liu et al. 2025 Supplementary Dataset (Round 2 Finding)

Two low-cost alternate access routes (recommended by explorer-critic) were attempted this round, before finalizing the "no dataset" conclusion from Round 1:

1. **PMC linkout check** — confirmed absent. No PMC record exists for PMID 40633848; IJAA did not trigger an NIH-mandated deposit for this article.
2. **Elsevier supplementary-materials CDN pattern** (`ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-{pii}-mmc{n}.{ext}`) — **this route succeeded**, and materially changes the assessment:
   - `1-s2.0-S0924857925001256-mmc1.docx` → HTTP 200, ~1.6 MB Word document. Content appears to be supplementary methods / PRISMA checklist / search-strategy material (not fully parsed line-by-line in this pass — worth a dedicated read-through before the extraction stage).
   - `1-s2.0-S0924857925001256-mmc2.pdf` → HTTP 200, ~625 KB. **This file is the actual patient/case-level extraction dataset**, evidently exported from an Excel workbook. It contains:
     - A **~324-row case-level table** (one row per patient-case-episode across the review's ~130 included studies), with fields including `case_id`, `report_id`, `reference` (citation string), `inf_site_main` (infection site), `bacteria_species_target` (pathogen — including many rows explicitly coded `"pa"` for *P. aeruginosa*), `age`, `sex`, `phage_route` (topical/IV/nebulized/oral/etc.), `phage_composition` (single vs. cocktail), `phage_abx_co` (co-administered with antibiotics — a direct modality-stratification field), `cli_impr` (clinical improvement, binary), `bacteria_erad` (bacterial eradication, 3-level), `ad_event` (adverse events), and `phage_resis` (resistance emergence) — a near-complete match to this project's own extraction-form schema (see `data_dictionary.md` §1).
     - Additional sheets: **RCT RoB2** risk-of-bias assessments for 9 RCTs, **ROBINS-I** assessments for 2 comparative cohort studies, and **NHLBI/JBI checklists** for case-series and case-report quality — i.e., Liu et al.'s full risk-of-bias grading is also included, not just outcome counts.
     - Several sheets on immunological/PK sub-analyses (cytokine "immune breakout," phage pharmacokinetics "PK breakout," neutralizing-antibody "neutralization breakout," "antiphage antibody breakout") — not directly relevant to this project's primary outcomes, but potentially useful for a mechanism/discussion subsection.
   - Critically, **many case-level rows in `mmc2.pdf` are drawn from studies already on this project's own extraction-target list** — e.g., Pirnay 2024, Green 2023, Aslam 2019/2020, Köhler 2023, Petrovic-Fabijan 2020 — meaning this file directly overlaps this review's own primary-study set for the *P. aeruginosa* subgroup specifically.

**Reuse implications (flagged for the strategist, not decided here):** This dataset is a genuine shortcut candidate, but using it is not a purely mechanical decision. Liu et al.'s categorical codings (e.g., `cli_impr` 0/1, `bacteria_erad` 0/1/2) reflect *their* operational definitions, which may not match this project's own pre-specified outcome definitions (see the research spec's Open Question on heterogeneous "clinical success" definitions). Three possible uses exist, in ascending order of methodological risk: (a) use `mmc2.pdf` purely to **identify which of the ~130 included studies have extractable *P. aeruginosa*-specific arms**, then extract independently from the primary sources; (b) use it as an **independent cross-validation check** against this project's own extraction; (c) **directly adopt** Liu et al.'s case-level codings for overlapping studies to save extraction time, with disclosure. Option (c) blurs the line between an independent systematic review and a re-analysis of a prior team's dataset and should not be adopted without the strategist's and the user's explicit sign-off. **At minimum, this file is an extremely valuable time-saver for scoping which of the ~130 Liu et al. included studies are *P. aeruginosa*-relevant**, and should be reviewed in full before extraction begins.

**Bottom line on Part 2 (revised):** Liu et al. 2025's supplementary materials — reachable via the Elsevier CDN direct-link route even though the main article is paywalled — **do** offer a reusable, case-level structured dataset, reversing the prior round's negative finding. Uyttebroek et al. 2022 (narrative review) and El Haddad et al. 2019 (case-series review) still offer no reusable dataset; those two conclusions are unchanged. **Resourcing implication for the strategist/coder:** extraction workload for the *P. aeruginosa* subgroup may be meaningfully reduced by cross-referencing `mmc2.pdf` against this project's own study list, but the degree of reliance on it (scoping tool vs. cross-check vs. direct adoption) is a strategy decision, not an access decision, and should be made explicitly before the coder builds the extraction spreadsheet.
